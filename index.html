Official Rules

KSB League Rules

This is the single source of truth for bankrolls, wagers, power-ups, loans, interest, postseason limits, parlays, and settlement.

1. Starting Bankroll & Champion

  • Every player starts with $2,000 in fake money.
  • The season runs through the Super Bowl.
  • Final standings are based on Net Worth = Cash − Principal Owed − Interest Owed.
  • There are no tiebreakers. Equal final net worth means a shared place/title.

2. Weekly Betting Limits

  • Maximum 10 wagers per NFL week.
  • Regular-season normal wagers: $10–$100.
  • One weekly Big Bet: $101–$200.
  • A parlay counts as one wager toward the weekly 10-bet limit.
  • Multiple wagers on the same NFL game are allowed.

3. Parlays

  • Parlays are allowed with no overall leg limit.
  • The site currently allows one leg per NFL game. Same-game parlays are not offered.
  • Parlay odds lock when the wager is submitted.
  • If any leg loses, the parlay loses.
  • If a leg pushes, that leg is removed and the remaining parlay odds are recalculated.
  • If every leg pushes, the stake is returned.

4. Weekly Power-Ups

  • Small Boost: +50% to the profit of one eligible wager.
  • Big Boost: +100% to the profit of one eligible wager.
  • Tax Man: target another player's eligible open wager before kickoff. If it wins, collect 20% of that wager's profit.
  • Only one Tax Man may be attached to a wager.
  • Big Bets cannot use Small Boost or Big Boost and are Tax Man exempt.
  • Power-ups reset each Tuesday morning and unused power-ups expire.
  • Voided/cancelled wagers restore an attached boost when applicable.

5. Loans & Interest

  • Loans are fixed at $1,000.
  • Maximum one new loan per NFL week.
  • There is no overall debt cap.
  • Interest is 10% per NFL week and is assessed Tuesday morning.
  • Newly borrowed principal gets one rollover of grace before becoming interest-bearing.
  • Available cash automatically pays Interest Owed first on Tuesday.
  • Any unpaid interest remains as Interest Owed.
  • Players may repay debt at any time. Repayments hit Interest Owed first, then Principal Owed.

6. Bet Locking & Settlement

  • No wager may be placed after kickoff.
  • Odds and lines lock when the wager is submitted.
  • There are no cash-outs or edits after submission.
  • Pushes return the original stake.
  • Voided/cancelled wagers return the stake.
  • Bad or missing lines are disabled rather than guessed.
  • Completed moneyline, spread, total, and parlay wagers are settled automatically from NFL score data.

7. Postseason & Super Bowl

  • During the NFL postseason before the Super Bowl, normal wager limits double to $20–$200.
  • Big Bet guardrails also double, up to $400.
  • Power-up strength stays the same during the postseason.
  • Super Bowl wagers have no wager amount limits.
  • No Small Boost, Big Boost, or Tax Man may be used on Super Bowl wagers.

8. Public Records & Standings

  • All wagers and transactions are visible league-wide.
  • The League Ledger records bets, settlements, loans, repayments, interest, Tax Man transfers, and commissioner actions.
  • The ledger is intended to be append-only. Corrections should be transparent rather than silently changing history.
  • Peak Net Worth and Lowest Net Worth are tracked for every player.

9. Tuesday Rollover

  • Charge weekly interest and auto-pay from available cash.
  • Save standings snapshots and update peak/low net worth.
  • Generate the weekly recap.
  • Reset the 10-bet counter, Big Bet availability, weekly loan slot, Small Boost, Big Boost, and Tax Man.

10. Commissioner Reset

  • The commissioner may reset the entire season from the Commissioner Controls panel.
  • A reset returns every player to $2,000, clears debt and records, and removes season wagers, ledger history, recaps, snapshots, and notifications.
  • The reset requires multiple confirmations because it cannot be undone.
Help Center

KSB FAQ

Basic help for placing bets, building parlays, using power-ups, understanding debt, and reading the standings.

How do I place a normal bet?

Open Sportsbook, click the line you want, enter your stake, choose Normal Bet, optionally select a power-up, then click Place Wager. A confirmation pop-up shows the stake and odds before submission.

How do I build a parlay?

Set Bet Format to Parlay, then click one selection from each NFL game you want to include. Add at least two legs, choose your stake, and submit the wager.

Why can't I add two picks from the same game?

KSB does not currently offer same-game parlays because correlated selections require special pricing. Regular parlays can use one leg from each game.

Does a parlay count as multiple weekly bets?

No. The entire parlay counts as one wager toward your 10-wager weekly limit.

When do odds update?

Odds refresh automatically around 12 PM and 8 PM Eastern. Everyone sees the same cached lines between refreshes.

When are bets settled?

The backend checks open wagers every hour. Once the score source reports a game final, eligible wagers are settled automatically.

What happens on a push?

A single-bet push returns the stake. On a parlay, the pushed leg is removed and the remaining parlay odds are recalculated. If every leg pushes, the full stake is returned.

What is the Big Bet?

Your Big Bet is the one weekly wager allowed above the normal regular-season maximum. It is $101–$200 during the regular season, cannot be boosted, and cannot be targeted by Tax Man.

How do Small Boost and Big Boost work?

Small Boost adds 50% to winning profit. Big Boost adds 100% to winning profit. Each is available once per NFL week on an eligible wager.

How does Tax Man work?

Use Tax Man on another player's eligible open wager before kickoff. If that wager wins, you receive 20% of its profit. If it loses or pushes, you receive nothing.

How do loans work?

You can borrow exactly $1,000 once per NFL week. The cash is added immediately and the same amount is added to Principal Owed.

When is interest charged?

Interest is charged Tuesday morning at 10% of interest-bearing principal. Newly borrowed principal gets one rollover of grace before it begins accruing interest.

How do debt repayments work?

You may repay at any time. Repayments apply to Interest Owed first and then Principal Owed. The site asks for confirmation before submitting a repayment.

What is Net Worth?

Net Worth = Cash − Principal Owed − Interest Owed. The standings are ranked by Net Worth, not simply by how much cash a player has.

What resets every Tuesday?

Your wager count, Big Bet, weekly loan slot, Small Boost, Big Boost, and Tax Man reset. Interest is processed and the weekly recap is generated at the same rollover.

Why did my wager leave Open Bets?

Once a wager settles, it is no longer considered open. The result and money movement remain in the League Ledger and feed into the weekly recap.

Can I edit or cancel a wager after placing it?

No. Submitted wagers cannot be edited or cashed out. Review the confirmation pop-up before committing the wager.

What if a bet settles incorrectly?

Contact the commissioner. The goal is to correct issues transparently rather than silently modifying league history.

What does Reset Entire Season do?

This commissioner-only action returns all players to $2,000 and clears season wagers, transaction history, recaps, snapshots, notifications, debt, and records. It requires multiple confirmations.

Where can I see everyone's activity?

Use League Ledger for league-wide money movements and Standings for bankroll, debt, records, ROI, peak/low net worth, Big Bet record, and Tax Man earnings.
