Official Rules

League Rules

This page is intended to be the single source of truth for the league.

1. Starting Bankroll & Champion

  • Every player starts with $2,000 in fake money.
  • The season runs through the Super Bowl.
  • Final standings are based on Net Worth = Cash − Principal Owed − Interest Owed.
  • There are no tiebreakers. Equal net worth means a shared place/title.

2. Weekly Betting Limits

  • Maximum 10 wagers per NFL week.
  • Regular-season normal wagers: $10–$100.
  • One weekly Big Bet: $101–$200.
  • Big Bet is tax-exempt and cannot use Small Boost or Big Boost.
  • During the NFL postseason, normal wager and Big Bet limits double.
  • Parlays are allowed with no additional leg limit.
  • Multiple wagers on the same game are allowed.

3. Weekly Power-Ups

  • Small Boost: +50% to the profit of one eligible wager.
  • Big Boost: +100% to the profit of one eligible wager.
  • Tax Man: target another player's eligible open wager before kickoff. If it wins, collect 20% of that wager's profit.
  • Tax Man cannot target a Big Bet.
  • Power-ups reset each Tuesday morning.
  • Postseason power-up strength stays the same.
  • Voided/cancelled bets restore any attached boost.

4. Super Bowl

  • Super Bowl wagers have no wager amount limits.
  • No Small Boost, Big Boost, or Tax Man may be used on Super Bowl wagers.

5. Loans & Interest

  • Loans are fixed at $1,000.
  • Maximum one loan per NFL week.
  • There is no overall debt cap.
  • Interest is 10% per NFL week and is assessed Tuesday morning.
  • A newly taken loan does not get charged interest until the following Tuesday rollover.
  • Available cash automatically pays weekly interest first.
  • Any unpaid interest becomes Interest Owed.
  • Players may repay debt at any time. Repayments hit Interest Owed first, then principal.

6. Bet Locking & Settlement

  • No wager may be placed after kickoff.
  • Odds and lines lock when the wager is submitted.
  • Pushes return the original stake.
  • Voided/cancelled bets return the stake.
  • Bad or missing lines are disabled rather than guessed.

7. Public Records

  • All wagers and transactions are visible league-wide.
  • Each player has a personal transaction history.
  • The League Ledger is append-only.
  • Commissioner corrections appear as public adjustment transactions rather than silently editing history.
  • Peak net worth and lowest net worth are tracked for every player.

8. Tuesday Rollover

  • Settle the previous week's activity.
  • Charge weekly interest and auto-pay from available cash.
  • Save standings snapshots and update peak/low net worth.
  • Generate the weekly recap.
  • Reset 10-bet limit, Big Bet availability, weekly loan availability, and weekly power-ups.
